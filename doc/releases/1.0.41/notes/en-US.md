Sally remains free, with every feature and update available to everyone.

- Clicking a server or network root in the Directory Line now opens the Network view. Share and folder links keep their usual navigation. (#155)
- Choose **On every startup** in **Options → Configuration → General → Check for updates** for an immediate background check at each launch. After a failed attempt, the next launch tries again. Existing interval choices keep their previous behavior. (#150)
- **Theme** and **Icon color** are grouped under **Application Appearance** on the **Appearance** page. Existing icon choices and saved preferences are retained. (#151)
